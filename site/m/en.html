NTP Time Sync Keeps the Nokia E72's clock right, over Wi-Fi
Download NTP Time Sync (.sisx) Needs a hacked phone (InstallServer patch).

Install

  1. If the phone has a SIM: turn off Network operator time (Ctrl. panel > Settings > General > Date and time).
  2. Download the .sisx above and install it to phone memory (C:).
  3. When the phone shows its access point list, pick your Wi-Fi. It syncs right away.
  4. Open NTP Time Sync from Applications to see the status, or choose Sync now.

Features

  • Wi-Fi only: no SIM needed, no mobile data used.
  • Syncs at boot, then every 6 hours (15 minutes to 1 week).
  • Asks the server 3 times and keeps the best answer.
  • Off by less than 0.5 s? It leaves the clock alone. It only sets UTC; your time zone stays.
  • Syncs again when something else moves the clock.
  • Screens in Vietnamese or English.

Good to know

  • Updating: if the installer says ntpsyncd is in use, open the app and choose Stop background sync; after installing, choose Start background sync.
  • Uninstalling: choose Stop background sync first.
  • Files: settings in C:\Data\ntpsyncd.ini, log in C:\Data\ntpsyncd.log. The log stays on the phone.
  • Can't download? The stock S60 browser only has TLS 1.0 and GitHub needs TLS 1.2. Install the TLS patch (nnproject.cc/tls), or download the file on a computer and copy it to the phone.
